About Mission Revenue Partners

More Mission, Less Admin

Mission Revenue Partners was born from a simple observation: too many incredible nonprofits and ministries spend more time on administrative tasks than on their actual mission.

Founded by Philip Raeburn, who leads Send Us International—an organization that sends young Christians on mission trips to Panama and runs Summer Gap Year programs—we understand firsthand the operational challenges that mission-focused organizations face.

Every hour spent on QuickBooks, donor management, email campaigns, or social media is an hour away from changing lives and advancing the Kingdom. That's why we exist.

Why We Started MRP

Running Send Us International taught us that great ministry organizations get bogged down by operations. We watched incredible leaders spend their evenings categorizing expenses instead of preparing for the mission field.

We saw passionate fundraisers manually tracking donations when they could be building relationships with donors. We witnessed gifted communicators struggling with email campaigns instead of crafting life-changing messages.

That's when we realized: What if there was a way for organizations to get professional-level operations support without hiring full-time staff?

"Every system we've built for Send Us International becomes a service we can offer to other mission-focused organizations. Our operational efficiency becomes your operational freedom."
